Demo Stays Roadmap V1
Purpose

The first version of Little Atlas needs a curated demo dataset that is large enough to validate:

Collection Engine
Family Intelligence
Destination Pages
Stay Pages
Internal Linking
SEO Landing Pages

The goal is not completeness.

The goal is coverage.

Every stay should strengthen one or more Family Intelligence use cases.
